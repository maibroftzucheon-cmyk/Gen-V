ABOUT GEN V

A new generation of beauty.

Gen V is a platform created to connect people with talented hairstylists and inspire new possibilities in hair and beauty.

OUR STORY

Beauty is an art worth discovering.

Gen V was created to make discovering hairstylists and hairstyles easier.

From braids and natural hair to wigs and locs, Gen V brings different forms of Nigerian hairstyling together in one place.

Our goal is to create a space where hairstylists can showcase their artistry while people can discover styles that inspire them.
