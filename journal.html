Ideas for your hair journey.

Guides & Inspiration

Explore straightforward guides for caring for your hair and finding a style that feels like you.

HAIR CARE

Building a simple hair-care routine

Start with a gentle cleanse, keep hair moisturised, and protect it from unnecessary friction. Adjust the steps and products to suit your hair and scalp.

INSPIRATION

Finding a hairstyle that fits your style

Think about the look you enjoy, how much daily maintenance you prefer, and the time you can set aside before choosing your next style.

STYLING

Questions to ask your hairstylist

Ask about preparation, upkeep, timing, and pricing before you book. Sharing reference images can help you and your stylist agree on the result.

NATURAL HAIR

Caring for natural hair

Handle hair gently, use a conditioner that suits your needs, and detangle in manageable sections. A consistent, comfortable routine matters more than a long list of products.

SCALP CARE

Making scalp care part of your routine

Cleanse your scalp regularly and avoid styles or products that cause discomfort. If irritation persists, seek advice from a qualified health professional.

PRODUCTS

Choosing styling products

Choose products for the finish and hold you want, and follow their directions. Start with a small amount and adjust to your hair's response.

PROTECTIVE STYLING

Keeping protective styles comfortable

Protective styles should not cause ongoing pain or excessive tension. Keep your scalp comfortable, follow your stylist's care guidance, and give your hair breaks between styles.

TOOLS

Using hair tools thoughtfully

Choose tools suited to your routine, keep them clean, and use heat carefully. Lower heat and fewer passes can help reduce unnecessary stress on hair.
